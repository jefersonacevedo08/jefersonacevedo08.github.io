01About

My work sits where physics meets deep learning: I build models and tools for hyperspectral and thermal imagery — radiative-transfer simulation with MODTRAN, atmospheric compensation in the LWIR, and segmentation of greenhouse-gas plumes in satellite data.

02Publications

Both papers were published at the IEEE International Geoscience and Remote Sensing Symposium (IGARSS 2026).

IGARSS 2026

Methane-Plume Segmentation from Hyperspectral Satellite Imagery via Multimodal Deep Learning

Brayan Quintero, Jeferson Acevedo, Samuel Traslaviña, Hoover Rueda-Chacón

A multimodal network that finds methane plumes in hyperspectral satellite scenes. It fuses a physics-based methane enhancement signal with transformer RGB features at several scales, beating previous results on MPDataset while staying light enough for large-scale monitoring.

  • Methane Detection

IGARSS 2026 Oral

Set-Based Transformer for Atmospheric Compensation in Standoff LWIR Hyperspectral Imaging

Fabian Perez, Nicolas Quintero, Jeferson Acevedo, Hoover Rueda-Chacón

Passive long-wave infrared sensors see every target through the atmosphere. We treat radiance captured at several standoff ranges as an unordered set and use a compact transformer to jointly estimate transmittance, path radiance and a shared downwelling spectrum — the terms needed to recover the target's own signal.

  • Atmospheric Compensation
